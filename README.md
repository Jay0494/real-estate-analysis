# 📊 Real Estate Sales & Rental Performance Analytics

## 📌 Overview

This project analyzes a multi-city real estate business to identify where demand exists, where revenue is lost, and how operations can be optimized across both **sales and rental markets**.

The core objective is to shift the business from **high-volume activity → high-efficiency revenue generation** by uncovering inefficiencies across the commercial and operational pipeline.

---

## ❓ Business Problem

Despite strong inbound demand, the business faces several performance challenges:

* Low conversion efficiency across the funnel
* Limited visibility into pipeline leakage
* Misalignment between market demand and property inventory
* Operational delays impacting customer experience and deal closure

---

## 🎯 Core Business Question

**Where should the business prioritize its efforts across cities, property types, and transaction types (rent vs sales) to maximize conversion efficiency, reduce time-to-sale, and capture the highest value demand?**

---

## 🧠 Analytical Approach

This analysis was structured as a **system-level evaluation**, not just a dashboard build.

### Framework:

* **Demand Layer** → Total inquiries
* **Conversion Layer** → Leads → Offers → Closed deals
* **Operational Layer** → Response time, Days on Market (DOM)
* **Market Layer** → City, Property Type, Transaction Type

## 💡 Recommendations

### 🚀 1. Reduce Response Time

 Target: **< 2 hours**
 Expected impact: Increased lead-to-offer conversion

---

### 🔧 2. Fix Funnel Leakage

 Improve lead qualification criteria
 Implement structured follow-up workflows

---

### 📦 3. Align Inventory with Demand

 Increase apartment listings in rental-heavy markets
 Reduce underperforming property types (e.g., land)

---

### 🌍 4. Market Prioritization

Focus rental strategy on high-demand cities:

 Chicago
 Austin
 Phoenix
 Denver

---

### 📊 5. Seasonal Optimization

 Increase marketing and listing activity in **Q4 peak periods**

---

## 🧠 How I Think as an Analyst

 Start with **decisions**, not data
 Focus on **where value is lost**, not just where activity exists
 Validate data before trusting insights
 Prioritize **high-impact drivers over vanity metrics**
 Always connect **insights → business actions → measurable outcomes**
